Library Rules

Security & Privacy

  • Do not share your login credentials with anyone.
  • Report any security concerns to the library staff immediately.
  • Users must not attempt to hack,damage or disrupt the library's systems.
  • Logging out after shared devices is recommended.

Borrowing & Returning

  • Return borrowed books on or before the due date.
  • Handle books with care to avoid damage.
  • Late returns may incur fines or penalties.
  • Renewals are subject to availability and library policies.

Conduct & Behavior

  • Posting offensive,misleading or inappropriate content is prohibited.
  • Follow the library's code of conduct and policies.
  • Respect the rights and privacy of other library users.
  • Report any disruptive behavior to the library staff.

About Us

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the library's operating hours?

The library is open Monday to Friday from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, and on Saturdays from 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM.

How can I borrow books from the library?

You can borrow books by registering for a library account and presenting your library card at the circulation desk.

Are there any fines for late returns?

Yes, late returns may incur fines. The specific fine amount and policies can be found in the library's borrowing guidelines.

Can I access the library's digital resources remotely?

Yes, registered library users can access digital resources such as e-books and online journals from anywhere with an internet connection.

How can I get assistance with research or finding information?

You can seek assistance from the library staff at the reference desk, or you can contact us via email or phone for research support.

What should I do if I encounter technical issues with the library's online services?

If you experience technical issues, please report them to the library's IT support team via email or phone. They will assist you in resolving the problem.
